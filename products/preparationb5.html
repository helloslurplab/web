About

Visible

barrier repair.

8% Panthenol & Phytosphingosine + 5% Artificial Human Tears

Beyond support. With a potent dose of Pro-Vitamin B5 and Phytosphingosine, Preparation B₅ provides instant barrier relief by delivering lipids (such as ceramides, cholesterol, and fatty acids) that make up the skin barrier, but more importantly boost your skin’s natural production of them. With a base made up of artificial human tears, each application readjusts the skin's pH to the level crucial for lipid production in the first place. This total combination strengthens and balances the skin barrier upon application, returning it to an ideal state from which to visibly transform thin, brittle skin surfaces, for good.

Breakouts, blemishes,

dryness—balanced at last.

Lactococcus Ferment Lysate + Glucosyl Hesperidin & Allantoin

Every concern, considered. Preparation B₅ gets to the root of the problem, efficiently targeting and reducing breakouts, blemishes, and sensitivity by balancing the skin's microbiome and oil production with Lactococcus Ferment Lysate and Linoleic Acid from Safflower Seed Oil. Polyquaternium-51 and Amylopectin mimic natural moisturizing factors that reduces transepidermal water loss (TEWL), enhancing hydration and barrier function. This improves skin plumpness and regulates oily, dry, or combination skin types. Antioxidants and soothing agents like Allantoin, Carnosine, and Glucosyl Hesperidin protect against environmental stress and aid in healing, resulting in a stronger skin barrier and making it easier to maintain a balanced complexion.

Heavyweight formulation.

Lightweight concentrate.

Microfluidizer Processor M-700

The microfluidizer machinery transforms Preparation B₅ from a potent concentrate to a highly potent concentrate. With extreme high pressure pushing the entire formulation through canals that are thinner than a strand of hair, Preparation B₅ exits from the opposite end featherlight, and not only more penetrable but, more bio-available.

Directions of Use

  1. Dispense a single pump of Preparation B₅ onto the palms and apply evenly to cleansed skin after Niacid or your preferred toner — morning and night.
  2. Complement Preparation B₅ with an ≥ SPF 50 sunscreen during the day to protect the healing barrier.

Tip: If you have a moderately to severely compromised barrier, Preparation B₅ is recommended as a standalone treatment for the first three weeks of use. After the initial application, apply a second pump of the formula both in the morning and at night.

Is Preparation B₅ recommended for teens?

Between the ages 9-19, the body experiences a surge in hormones causing the overproduction of sebum potentially harmful for the barrier. To treat a compromised barrier, acne and/or clogged pores, it is recommended to complement Preparation B₅ with Decalt per necessity.

Is Preparation B₅ suitable for sensitive skin?

Preparation B₅ is intently formulated to be welcomed by any skin type including hypersensitive.

What makes Preparation B₅ a Complete Concern Concentrate?

A compromised skin barrier is one of the unknown root causes behind skin concerns. By internally restoring a healthy barrier, Preparation B₅ creates a resilient and unlikely environment for a complete suite of concerns to recur.

Does it serve as a moisturizer?

Although possessing moisturizing properties, Preparation B₅ is primarily a barrier-restoring and complete concern addressing solution.

How does Preparation B₅ differ from other barrier-repairing products?

Rather than merely supplying the skin with components that make up the barrier, Preparation B₅ is designed to stimulate the skin's innate barrier-forming production and revitalize its repair and defense mechanisms.
